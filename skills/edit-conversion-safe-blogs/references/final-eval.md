# Conversion-safe final evaluation

Answer each check with pass or fail. Fix every failure before returning the
draft.

## Conversion contract

1. Is the article's primary job unchanged?
2. Is the intended reader action still obvious?
3. Does every protected block remain in its intended position?
4. Are the CTA heading, destination URLs, offer, positioning, proof, and
   capability claims preserved?
5. Has the edit avoided weakening the CTA with new hedging or neutral language?
6. Has intentional conversion repetition survived?
7. Did `conversion_guard.py verify` pass, or was the equivalent comparison
   completed manually for pasted text?
8. Is every structural or strategic change explicitly approved and reported?

## Editorial quality

1. Does the draft preserve the writer's voice and strong human lines?
2. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual prose problem?
3. Are claims concrete, sourced where needed, and free from invention?
4. Are filler, puffery, robotic patterns, tangled sentences, and decorative
   formatting fixed?
5. Does the ending retain the clearest next action rather than replacing it
   with a generic summary or clever kicker?

## Delivery

1. Does the response include the full edited draft or a direct file link?
2. Does it include **What changed**?
3. Does it include a **Conversion integrity** confirmation?
