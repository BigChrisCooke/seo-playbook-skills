# Prose rubric

Use this abbreviated rubric when the `no-ai-slop` skill is unavailable.

## Preserve

- The writer's point, vocabulary, cadence, humour, bluntness, uncertainty, and
  useful digressions
- Strong human sentences, concrete facts, numbers, mechanisms, and examples
- Intentional repetition that supports comprehension or conversion
- The existing structure unless it genuinely blocks understanding

## Improve

- Lead with the point when setup adds nothing.
- Prefer active voice, direct verbs, specific nouns, and clear sentence logic.
- Split genuinely tangled sentences without making every paragraph equally
  tidy.
- Remove unsupported claims or ask for a source. Do not invent evidence.

## Cut or rewrite

- Generic throat-clearing and faux-insight setups
- Binary contrasts, negative lists, and self-answered rhetorical questions
- Importance puffery, weasel attribution, and abstract business jargon
- Dramatic fragments, robotic rhythm, decorative em dashes, and fake-profound
  endings
- Summary conclusions that merely repeat the article
- Decorative bold, emoji headings, and lists that would read better as prose

Common empty terms include: delve, leverage, utilise, facilitate, empower,
streamline, robust, cutting-edge, transformative, elevate, harness, "at the end
of the day", "in today's world", "it's important to note", and "let's dive in".

Make the minimum effective edit. A prose cleanup must not become an excuse to
remove the article's commercial purpose.
